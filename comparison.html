Proposal 1: MVP Economy

Best for: Quick market validation, limited budget, simple business model

Ideal if: You want to test the market quickly, have budget constraints, or prefer a simple solution to start with

Risk: May require significant additional investment later for scaling

Proposal 2: Phased Enterprise

Best for: Long-term business growth, professional operations, competitive advantage

Ideal if: You're committed to the business long-term, want professional-grade tools, and can invest in quality

Risk: Higher upfront investment, longer time to initial launch

Recommendation

Choose Proposal 1 (MVP Economy) if you need quick market validation, have budget constraints, or want to minimize initial risk. However, budget for potential rebuilds when scaling.

Choose Proposal 2 (Phased Enterprise) if you're serious about building a professional, scalable business and can invest in quality from the start. The phased approach allows you to start generating revenue after 12 weeks while building advanced capabilities.
